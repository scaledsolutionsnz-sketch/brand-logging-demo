Who We Are

Locally Owned.
Professionally Run.

Brand Logging is a North Canterbury forestry contractor with deep roots in the Sefton community. We specialise in the full spectrum of forest harvesting — from planning and felling through to extraction and haulage — for landowners, farmers, and commercial forest managers.

Our team brings years of hands-on experience and modern machinery to every job, delivering safe, efficient, and land-conscious harvesting that gets results. Whether you've got a small woodlot or a large commercial block, we treat every site with the same level of care.

Certified operators with full H&S compliance
Well-maintained modern harvesting equipment
Full public liability & contractor's insurance
Servicing Sefton, Rangiora & wider Canterbury
What We Do

Complete Forest
Harvesting Services

From the first tree down to the last log loaded, we manage the full harvest with precision and care for the land beneath it.

🌲

Forest Harvesting

Full-scale commercial harvesting using modern felling and forwarding equipment. Efficient, safe, and delivered to road on schedule.

🪓

Tree Felling & Processing

Manual and mechanical felling for all species and site types. Our operators are trained to handle complex and steep terrain safely.

🚛

Log Hauling & Transport

Coordinated haulage from forest to mill, working closely with trusted transport partners to keep your timber moving on time.

🏗️

Land Clearing

Clearing shelter belts, windbreaks, and scrub for farming or development. We leave your land clean, tidy, and ready for its next purpose.

⚙️

Mechanical Extraction

Purpose-built extraction machinery for challenging terrain — maximising timber recovery while minimising ground disturbance.

Site Assessment & Quoting
